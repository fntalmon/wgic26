Innovation Day — 30 October
Spend a day inside one of the real sites of a three-year, EU-funded research project — not a set-up staged for the occasion, but the actual green roof and monitoring systems BIG4LIFE has spent three years installing and monitoring at the Universitat de Lleida's Agrobiotech Park.
Participants can choose between two hands-on workshops covering different facets of urban green infrastructure, followed by lunch at the park and a guided afternoon through Lleida's historic quarter.
Workshop 1: Bugs, Cities and Green RoofsEnglish - SpanishSmall creatures, big impact. Discover the hidden world above our cities, where urban ecology meets real-world action. Learn how arthropods help make cities healthier, greener and more resilient, guided by the very researchers studying urban biodiversity on Lleida's monitored rooftops.
Workshop 2: Good Practices and Maintenance of Green InfrastructureSpanish - EnglishLearn hands-on from four companies that install and maintain these systems every day — not case studies, but the people who actually do the work. Discover what makes a green infrastructure project succeed and last: proven construction methods, innovative materials, and the mistakes to avoid, explained by those who've gone through them on site.
