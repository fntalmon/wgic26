We are thrilled to announce a unique opportunity for WGIC26 authors: the best papers presented at the congress will be **fast-tracked for publication in Special Issues of Urban Forestry & Urban Greening (Elsevier)**.

## About the Journal

**Urban Forestry & Urban Greening** is one of the leading scientific journals in the field of urban green infrastructure and nature-based solutions. Published by Elsevier, it provides an international platform for high-quality research that bridges the gap between science, policy, and practice.

## What This Means for Authors

This collaboration offers several key advantages:

- **Fast-track review process** for selected WGIC26 papers
- **International visibility** among researchers, practitioners, and policymakers
- **Rigorous peer-review process** ensuring high academic standards
- **Impactful contributions** to one of the most respected journals in the field

## Submission Areas

Papers covering the following themes are particularly encouraged:

- Urban forest management and ecosystem services
- Green roof and wall performance and design
- Nature-based solutions for climate adaptation
- Biodiversity in urban environments
- Human health and well-being benefits of urban greening
- Policy and governance frameworks for green infrastructure

## How to Participate

To be considered for the Special Issue:

1. **Submit your abstract** through the WGIC26 submission portal by the deadline
2. **Present your research** at the congress in Barcelona (27–30 October 2026)
3. **Selected authors** will be invited to submit a full paper for fast-track review

All submissions will undergo the same rigorous peer-review process that has made Urban Forestry & Urban Greening a benchmark publication in the field.

## A Unique Opportunity

This partnership reflects WGIC26's commitment to not only sharing knowledge at the congress, but also ensuring that groundbreaking research reaches the broader scientific community through established, high-impact channels.
