**Gabriel Pérez**, Chair of WGIC26 and member of the Scientific Committee, recently represented the congress at **REBUILD 2026**, one of the leading events in the building and construction sector.

## Session: "Green in Hospitality Spaces"

Gabriel Pérez took part in the session **"Green in hospitality spaces"**, alongside **Jeffrey Brock**, Founding Partner of Moneo Brock. The discussion brought together perspectives from research, architecture, and industry to explore the transformative potential of nature in hospitality environments.

## Key Discussion Points

The session explored how green infrastructure and nature-based solutions can enhance hospitality environments, contributing to:

- **More sustainable spaces** — Reducing energy consumption and environmental impact through integrated vegetation
- **Resilient design** — Climate-adaptive strategies that perform under changing conditions
- **User-focused experiences** — Biophilic environments that improve guest well-being and satisfaction
- **Economic value** — How green credentials increasingly drive hospitality industry competitiveness

## Connecting Research, Industry, and Practice

This presence at REBUILD 2026 reinforces WGIC26's role as a bridge between:

- Cutting-edge research in green infrastructure
- Real-world industry applications across sectors
- Policy frameworks that enable sustainable development

The hospitality sector represents a growing area of opportunity for green infrastructure implementation, with hotels, resorts, and urban developments increasingly integrating living systems into their design.
