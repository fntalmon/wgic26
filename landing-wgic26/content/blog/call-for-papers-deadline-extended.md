The World Green Infrastructure Congress (WGIC26) has officially extended its Call for Papers & Projects deadline, responding to the significant international interest received in recent weeks.

## New Deadline: 31 May 2026

This extension provides a valuable opportunity for researchers, professionals, institutions, and innovators from around the world to prepare and submit their contributions. The congress will take place from **27–29 October at CCIB Barcelona** and on **30 October at Universitat de Lleida**.

## Topics of Interest

We welcome submissions related to:

- **Green Infrastructure** — Design, implementation, and management of living systems in urban environments
- **Nature-Based Solutions** — Ecosystem services and biodiversity integration
- **Urban Resilience** — Climate adaptation and sustainable city planning
- **Health & Well-being** — Biophilic design and human-centric green spaces
- **Global Collaboration** — Cross-border knowledge exchange and partnerships
- **Sustainability & Innovation** — Emerging technologies and best practices

## Why Submit?

Presenting at WGIC26 offers:

- International visibility among leading experts in green infrastructure
- Networking opportunities with researchers, policymakers, and industry leaders
- The best papers will be fast-tracked for publication in **Special Issues of Urban Forestry & Urban Greening (Elsevier)**
- A platform to showcase innovative projects and case studies to a global audience

## How to Submit

👉 Submit your paper or project at: [https://wgic26.barcelona](https://wgic26.barcelona)

Don't miss this extended opportunity to share your work with the global green infrastructure community. We look forward to receiving your contributions!
